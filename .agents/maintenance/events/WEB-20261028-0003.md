# WEB-20261028-0003: Keep Menu glass resources valid across disclosure toggles

## Summary
Prevent closed native disclosure geometry from replacing the valid open Menu glass mask and refraction resources.

## Reason
HC-232 Owner footage showed full navigation over a narrow backing strip. Runtime samples confirmed full-width open glass temporarily used a narrow mask generated while details was closed, including cases without text selection.

## Actions
Skip closed-panel resource measurement. Refresh resources on the open attribute mutation before paint, cancel pending animation-frame refresh, and disconnect the observer on pagehide. Preserve selection, geometry, scrolling, focus and accepted glass material.

## Validation
A new behavioral regression failed against the predecessor and passes after repair. All 18 applicable Home/Search/disclosure tests and the normal 137-route build passed. Ordinary and native Chrome Page zoom 200% resource samples have zero open mask/geometry mismatches after repair. Three identical-protocol Lighthouse 12.8.2 runs yielded median LCP 2189.2725 ms, CLS 0.0112339471, TBT 0 ms and Performance 99.

## Result
The isolated local successor removes the reproduced mask lifecycle failure and preserves the existing performance budgets. Independent exact-successor review and browser evidence are recorded separately.

## Rollback
Revert this scoped successor commit. Frozen predecessor source, output and evidence remain unchanged.

## Follow-up
Reconcile exact-successor Menu toggle/resize/native-zoom evidence in HC-232. Do not infer independent Windows contrast or IME execution from this repair.
