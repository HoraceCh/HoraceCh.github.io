## Project scope

This is the team's RoboMaster 2026 engineering robot project. The public repository includes mechanical CAD, fabrication drawings, a bill of materials, and separate arm and chassis firmware projects.

## My contribution

As a core team member, I contributed to wheel design, assembly, and robot integration. The team repository's firmware and other engineering artifacts are not a claim that I independently authored the complete robot or its control systems.

## Status and recognition

The team received the following recognition:

- **2026-08:** 第二十五届全国大学生机器人大赛 RoboMaster 2026 机甲大师超级对抗赛全国赛三等奖 — 国家级三等奖；团队（主力队员）。
- **2026-06:** 第二十五届全国大学生机器人大赛 RoboMaster 2026 机甲大师超级对抗赛区域赛（东部赛区）二等奖 — 省部级二等奖；团队（主力队员）。

Issuer for both records: 全国大学生机器人大赛组织委员会.

Competition participation is complete. The repository remains an ongoing collection of engineering materials, rather than a complete, directly deployable product.
